Research — Master's Thesis

Cross-Domain Stability of Explainable AI Models
NUMPY · SCIKIT-LEARN · SHAP · LIME · ONGOING
Designed an end-to-end pipeline evaluating whether SHAP and LIME explanations stay stable across domains (healthcare vs. finance) — not just whether a model is accurate, but whether its reasoning can be trusted to generalize. Proposed a novel Domain Stability Index combining rank-based, cosine similarity, and variance-based metrics, with statistical rigor from bootstrap confidence intervals and Welch's t-tests — methodology directly transferable to A/B testing and experimentation.
